   READ THIS BEFORE ACTING ON ANY ROW ABOVE.
   - None of these configs has a price target (rr=0). The exit is 'price is back at
     the mean it left'. A position wins unless it first travels the whole stop - 12
     ATR(5m) - against it, so a high hit rate is the shape of the trade, not proof
     of a signal. The unfiltered column is what that shape alone earns.
   - The 70% only exists at rr=0. At rr=0.5, where a target is 0.5R and the null is
     already 66.7%, ZERO of 265 looks cleared 70% when 74 should have from worthless
     signals. With a real target the strategy does not just miss the bar, it sits
     under it. There is no pair here that wins 70% of the time for a payoff.
   - Costs are the whole story on this frame: every one of these pairs falls below
     70% when the spread is doubled, and the two with the best expectancy go flat.
     A 5m mean-reversion edge of well under a pip per trade does not survive a
     slightly bad fill, a news day, or an ECN that is not quoted at the median.
